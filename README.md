
# STEMHER Africa

STEMHER Africa is an early-stage initiative working to make STEM education and opportunities more accessible to girls.

The initiative focuses on **practical STEM learning, mentorship, career exposure, leadership, and access to resources** that help girls explore STEM beyond the careers they traditionally encounter.

This repository contains the landing page for STEMHER Africa and serves as an early digital home for the initiative as the platform and its programs continue to develop.

### Focus Areas

* 🔬 Practical STEM education
* 👩🏾‍💻 Girls and women in STEM
* 🤝 Mentorship and role models
* 🚀 STEM career exposure
* 🌍 Accessible STEM opportunities across Africa
* 💡 Technology, innovation, and digital learning

### Status

**Early stage / prototype**

The landing page is part of the ongoing development of STEMHER Africa. The initiative is being shaped through hands-on work with girls, educators, mentors, and STEM practitioners.
